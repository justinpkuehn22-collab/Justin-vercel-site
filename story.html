My story

Building is the passion.

I start companies because building is what I love. Here's how I went from co-founding a company in college to building apps, working in product and selling.

2017

Arizona State University

Starting at ASU

In 2017 I started at Arizona State in Technological Entrepreneurship and Management, a joint program between the W.P. Carey School of Business and the Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering. I was passionate about finance and tech from the start. In early December 2019 I called the pandemic recession before it hit, and that whiteboard is from when we really started cooking up our plays.

W.P. Carey School of BusinessFulton Schools of Engineering
2021

Started in college · Intuico Digital

Co-founding Intuico while still in school

While I was still at ASU, I co-founded Intuico Digital with my business partner. It's a boutique consultancy that builds whatever a business needs: custom software, websites, apps and marketing. It's still running today.

Through Intuico we've built over 200 websites and launched our own products, including GoAutoFire, Trydrobe and Bloom. We built the business from the ground up, and now we're scaling it to the level we always knew it could reach.

2021–23

Triangle / DoorScore · Director of Operations

Apps and 17+ patents in real-estate tech

After graduation I joined Triangle / DoorScore as Director of Operations. I helped build the company's apps, including a mobile app that made the rental market more transparent, and we onboarded over 3,000 users in the first two months after launch.

I also created 17+ patents. Most are in real-estate tech, and some reach well beyond it into broader technology.
